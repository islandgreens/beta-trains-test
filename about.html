About The Beta Train

Finding out about video game betas is harder than it should be. Sign-ups get buried in press cycles, forum threads vanish, and by the time most people hear "beta," it's already closed.

This is a calendar, nothing more complicated than that. PlayStation, Xbox, and PC betas for games worth paying attention to, sorted by when you can actually still act on them.

What gets listed

Notable, press-covered titles you'd find on Steam or a major console storefront. Not mobile games, not browser games, not obscure playtests nobody's heard of.

How it's curated

Every entry is checked by hand against an official source before it goes up.

Built as a passion project and still actively evolving, so expect some rough edges along the way.
